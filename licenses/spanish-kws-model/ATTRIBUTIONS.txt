MODEL ATTRIBUTION
=================

Model:
TKA KWS — Spanish (Zipformer transducer)

Model repository:
https://huggingface.co/jguerrisi/sherpa-onnx-kws-zipformer-es-3M-2026-08-08

The model card identifies the following technologies and resources:

1. sherpa-onnx
   Project: https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx
   Role: Keyword spotting inference and model compatibility.

2. icefall
   Project: https://github.com/k2-fsa/icefall
   Role: Model training recipe and Zipformer transducer approach.

3. Multilingual LibriSpeech (MLS)
   Role: Spanish speech training data.
   The model card identifies MLS as a training resource and indicates
   that it is derived from LibriVox.
   Dataset information: https://www.openslr.org/94/

4. MUSAN
   Role: Noise augmentation during training.
   Dataset information: https://www.openslr.org/17/

5. Piper voices
   Role: Synthetic speech augmentation for keyword training.
   The model card reports using multiple Piper voices.
   Project: https://github.com/rhasspy/piper
